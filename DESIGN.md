# Design

## Direction

A modern dark technical-operations world for a support specialist. The interface uses near-black surfaces, high-contrast sans-serif typography, orange status accents, and console-like structured details.

## Palette

- Ink: #0B0C0E
- Surface: #111317
- Surface 2: #17191D
- Paper: #F4F5F1
- Orange: #FF6534
- Orange bright: #FF7749
- Muted: #A9ADB5

## Typography

- Display, headings, and interface: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- Supporting labels and data: SFMono, Consolas, Liberation Mono, monospace
- Headings use tight negative tracking and strong weight.
- Body copy uses a readable modern sans-serif.

## Layout

- A narrow persistent left rail on wide screens
- 62/38 hero split
- Alternating light, orange, and dark full-width bands
- Timeline rows rather than nested cards
- Two-column capability grid on desktop and one-column grid on mobile

## Interaction

- Direct text links and mailto actions
- Sharp rectangular buttons with slight offset shadows
- Subtle 180ms hover and motion transitions
- Reduced-motion support
- Visible keyboard focus
